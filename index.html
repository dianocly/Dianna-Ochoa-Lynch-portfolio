Mechanical Engineering · MIT Class of 2028

Hi, I’m Dianna E. Ochoa Lynch.

I am an undergraduate mechanical engineering student at MIT, specializing in robotics and mechanism design. My work spans actuator components at Boston Dynamics, tendon-driven robotics research at MIT CSAIL, and competition robots.

I enjoy taking ideas all the way from early pencil sketches through parametric CAD, finite-element analysis, and fabrication on manual tools and CNC equipment. I iterate quickly, test aggressively, and care about systems that are robust, precise, elegant, and easy to build and maintain.

MIT GPA: 4.9/5.0 · B.S. Mechanical Engineering, expected May 2028.
Relevant coursework: Mechanics and Materials II, Thermofluids Engineering II (in progress), Design and Manufacturing II, Dynamics and Controls II (in progress), Numerical Computation.
